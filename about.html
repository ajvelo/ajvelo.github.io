About

Hi, I'm Andreas.

Flutter enthusiast and seasoned developer with a passion for building stunning, responsive and intuitive apps that deliver a seamless user experience.

Most of my work is in Flutter and Dart. I like apps whose architecture you can explain on a whiteboard: clear layers, small use cases, and tests written first so refactoring stays cheap. Several of my open-source projects exist purely to show one of those ideas end to end, and I write about them on Medium.

Before Flutter I built native iOS apps in Swift, and a MERN-stack web app or two. That background is why I care about platform details: an app should feel at home on the device it runs on.

What I'm working on now

AI coding tools are fast but overconfident. I've been building tooling that makes them earn trust: ForgeStack makes agents critique each other before proposing a change, and claude-toolkit gives Claude Code skills, safety hooks and a ticket pipeline that never commits, pushes or opens a PR without approval.
